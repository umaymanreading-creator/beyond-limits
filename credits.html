Beyond Limits

Original supplementary learning activities for Grade 1, aligned to the vocabulary, phonics and grammar topics in Look 1, Bahrain Edition, by Katherine Bilsborough and Steve Bilsborough (National Geographic Learning). Original examples and games are used; the textbook and its photographs are not reproduced here.

Phoneme recordings

Recorded phonics sounds from Buzzphonics, Debbie Dann, supplied under the MIT licence. Recordings are used without modification. The letter k uses the /k/ recording provided as c; q uses the qu recording. This teaches sounds, rather than spoken letter names. A letter may represent other sounds in words beyond these introductory examples.

Full phonics licence

Voice and illustrations

Whole-word and example-sentence audio is prepared with the English (UK) female Sonia voice. All illustrations were generated specifically for this project. Human review by the teaching team is welcome before classroom-wide use.

Handwriting font

Andika, an educational font from SIL, uses clear single-storey lowercase forms. SIL Open Font License.

Your progress

Only the first name or nickname, class, role and earned stars are saved in this browser. Names, classes, roles and points are submitted to the school’s separate English register. Speaking uses automatic en-GB browser speech recognition. No recording is stored or replayed by this site; the browser provider may process speech online. Only progress points are submitted to the school, not audio or transcripts. Letters use listening and tracing. Automatic speech checks are available for word practice only. Changing browsers or clearing browser data can remove local progress. Use the progress download to keep your own copy.
